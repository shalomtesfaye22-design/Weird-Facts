Weird Facts About the Bermuda Triangle

Swipe, scroll, or use the scrollbar below to explore the ocean mysteries

01

The Bermuda Triangle is bounded by Miami, Bermuda, and Puerto Rico.

02

Some pilots have reported compass malfunctions in the area.

03

Methane gas eruptions have been proposed as one explanation for disappearances.

04

The region experiences sudden and powerful storms.

05

The triangle covers roughly 500,000 square miles of ocean.

06

Some ships reported strange fogs before disappearing.

07

The famous Flight 19 vanished there in 1945.

08

Many disappearances have later been explained by weather or human error.

09

The U.S. government does not officially recognize the Bermuda Triangle as a dangerous zone.

10

Despite its reputation, many studies suggest the area is no more dangerous than other heavily traveled ocean regions.
