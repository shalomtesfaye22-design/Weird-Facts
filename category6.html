Weird Facts About Aliens

Swipe, scroll, or use the scrollbar below to explore extraterrestrial mysteries

01

No scientifically verified alien has ever been discovered.

02

The term "alien" usually refers to intelligent extraterrestrial life.

03

Many popular depictions of aliens as grey-skinned beings with large black eyes originated in 20th-century UFO reports.

04

The famous 1947 incident in Roswell helped spark modern alien conspiracy theories.

05

Some scientists believe alien life, if it exists, could look completely different from anything on Earth.

06

The Fermi Paradox asks: if aliens are common, where is everybody?

07

Reports of alien abductions became especially popular during the 1960s and 1970s.

08

Scientists have proposed that advanced aliens might communicate using methods humans have not yet discovered or understood.

09

Some theories suggest highly advanced aliens could appear more machine than biological.

10

If aliens exist and are much older than humanity, their technology could seem like magic to us.
