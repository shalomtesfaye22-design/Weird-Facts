Weird Facts About the Nephilim

Swipe, scroll, or use the scrollbar below to explore ancient biblical mysteries

01

The Nephilim are mentioned in the Bible in Genesis 6:4.

02

The word "Nephilim" is often translated as "giants," though its exact meaning is debated.

03

According to one interpretation, the Nephilim were the offspring of the "sons of God" and the "daughters of men."

04

The Nephilim are described as mighty warriors and men of renown.

05

They are mentioned again in the biblical Book of Numbers, where spies report seeing giant descendants in Canaan.

06

Ancient texts outside the Bible, such as the Book of Enoch, expand their story and describe them as enormous giants.

07

Some legends claim the Nephilim were so large that they towered over ordinary humans.

08

The true identity of the Nephilim has been debated by scholars, theologians, and historians for centuries.

09

Some modern conspiracy theories claim giant skeletons discovered around the world are evidence of the Nephilim, although no verified evidence supports these claims.

10

The Nephilim remain one of the most mysterious and controversial figures in ancient religious literature.
